**What is Financial System?**<br>
The Financial system deals with the transactions and flow of money between _Savers, investors, lenders, and borrowers._ It is an interconnected network between _Banks, Stock Markets, Businessess, Insurance companies, Payment system, and regulations which moves  money through an economy_.

**Money, Credit and Finance**<br>
Money, Credit and Finance is what a financial system concerend off.

- _Money_ is a basic medium of exchange and means of payments.
- _Credit_ is purchasing power pulled from the future into the present, based purely on trust(the word itself comes from the Latin _credre_ means "to believe").<br>
When we buy something with credit, the transactions are _not_ settled. Which means we basically aquire an asset in exchange for a liability(debt). And we are making promise to settle the transactions later using actual money, usally with a panely(interest) for the privilege of accessing that value early.
- _Finance_ is the system which moves and direct money and credit over time. It is the study of capital Allocation, which involves borrowing, lending, investing , assessing risk and forecasting.
