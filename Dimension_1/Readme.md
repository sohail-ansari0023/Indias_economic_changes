<h1 style='text-align: center'><b>Dimension 1 - Macroeconomic Performance</b></h1>

#### **Main Question**:
> **Did India's economy become substanially larger between May 2004-May 2014 and May 2014-May 2024?**

How we're gonna find out? I've dissected it into these subquestions:

1. **How did India's GDP change in absolute terms?**
   - GDP at the beginning and end of each period
   - Absolute increase in GDP
   - Percentage increase in   GDP
   - How large was the economy at the end of each period?

This answers: **"How much bigger did the economy become?"**

But there's an important trap here: GDP measured in current US dollars can be affected by inflation and exchange-rate movements. So we shouldn't use it alone.

2. **How fast did India's real GDP grow?**
   - Annual real GDP gowth rate
   - Average growth rate during each period
   - Compound growth of real GDP
   - Compare the growth trajectory rather than only the starting and ending values

This answers: **"Was the economy actually producing substantially more goods and services, after removing the effect of changing prices?"**

This is probably the most important GDP question.

3. **How did GDP per capita change?**
   - GDP per capita at the beginning and end
   - Percentage change
   - Real GDP per capita, where appropriate
   - Compare GDP growth with population growth

This is crucial because this might answers: **Total GDP can grow rapidly while GDP per person grows much more slowly.**

For example, if GDP grows 70% but population grows 25%, the experience at the individual level is different from simply saying "GDP grew 70%."

4. **Was economic growth consistent or vaolatile?** For each period:
   - Annual GDP growth rate
   - Mean/average growth
   - Standard deviation of growth
   - Maximum growth rate
   - Minimum growth rate
   - Number of years with particularly weak growth

Then ask: **Was one period characterized by more stable growth, while the other experienced larger fluctuations?**

This is where we "more/less volatile" question becomes measurable rather than subjective.

5. **What did the growth trajectory actually look like?**

    Instead of comparing only two endpoints, we'll plot the entire 2004-2024 trajectory.

    We'll look for:
    - sustained acceleration
    - slowdown
    - unusually strong years
    - unusually weak years
    - major turning points
    - periods of recovery

This matters because two periods can have the same average growth but completely different trajectories.

6. **How much of GDP growth came from population growth versus output per person?**

This connects:

**Real GDP growth -> Population growth -> Real GDP per capita growth**

And help us to distingush:
> "India's economy expanded"

from 
> "Economic output available per person increased."

7. **Did the structure of economic growth change?**

I would add this as a supporting question, although we'll investigate it much more deeply in our later **Sectoral Transformation** dimension.

For this first dimension, we can briefly examine:
- Aggriculture's contribution
- Industry's contribution
- Services contribution

The deeper sector analysis can wait for that later dimension.

8. **How did India's economic size compare internationally?**

A useful supporting question:
> Did India's position in the world economy change during these periods?

Possible Indicators:
- India's share of world GDP
- GDP relative to major economies
- GDP measured using PPP

This gives us context. A country can grow substantially while the global economy is also changing.

9. **Did nomial GDP growth tell the same story as real GDP growth?**

This is an important analytical check. 

We'll deliberately compare:

**Nominal GDP -> Inflation/exchange-rate effects -> Real GDP**

Because otherwise we migh make the classic mistake:

> "GDP increased by x%, therefore the economy physically produced x% more."

That's not necessarily true.
